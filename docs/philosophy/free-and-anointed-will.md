# Free and anointed will

*God gave people the power to choose. AI assistance does not take that gift away, but a person can give it away.*

---

God made people in his image (Genesis 1:27). He put choices in front of them and told them to choose (Deuteronomy 30:19). A person's work carries their will into the world. That is part of what it means to make something.

So a Christian who works with AI has a practical question: how do you accept this help and still exercise your free and anointed will?

Free means that the choice is truly yours. No default, no habit and no machine made it for you.

Anointed means that you do not choose alone. A Christian asks God for wisdom, and listens, and brings that discernment into the work (James 1:5).

AI can offer you a thousand options. It cannot tell you which one you are called to make. That discernment stays with you.

This wiki argues from a Christian foundation, and it says so plainly. Its practical pages can help anyone who wants their work to be their own.
