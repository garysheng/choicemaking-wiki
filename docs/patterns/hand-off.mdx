**Family:** [Govern](/patterns). Decide how the deciding goes. **Example:** Let the AI choose this one.

## When to use it

Use it to give a choice to someone else.

## What a good design needs

A good design says who decides, what they know, and how you can take it back.

## How it fails

It fails when the AI decides and nobody handed it the choice.
