**Family:** [Govern](/patterns). Decide how the deciding goes. **Example:** Order one more test.

## When to use it

Use it when you do not know enough yet.

## What a good design needs

A good design shows what is known, what is missing, and what waiting costs.

## How it fails

It fails when the only options are yes and no.
