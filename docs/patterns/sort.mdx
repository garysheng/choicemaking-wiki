**Family:** [Select](/patterns). Choose among what exists. **Example:** Sort notes into themes.

## When to use it

Use it to put items into groups or slots.

## What a good design needs

A good design shows one item at a time, and it lets you make a new group.

## How it fails

It fails when the groups are fixed and nothing can fit outside them.
