**Family:** [Shape](/patterns). Change the work. **Example:** The verse from take 2 and the chorus from take 5.

## When to use it

Use it to take the best parts of several options.

## What a good design needs

A good design shows the parts in one grid and lets you swap any part.

## How it fails

It fails when you can only ask for "the second one with the first one's ending."
