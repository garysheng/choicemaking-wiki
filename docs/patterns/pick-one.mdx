**Family:** [Select](/patterns). Choose among what exists. **Example:** A brand direction.

## When to use it

Use it for two to five different options.

## What a good design needs

A good design shows them side by side, with the reason and the risk of each.

## How it fails

It fails when options arrive as a short list of names, or when they are not truly different.
