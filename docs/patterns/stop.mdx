**Family:** [Govern](/patterns). Decide how the deciding goes. **Example:** Stop an agent mid-task.

## When to use it

Use it to halt work in progress.

## What a good design needs

A good design keeps the button in reach, and it says what is done and what cannot be undone.

## How it fails

It fails when the button is hard to find, or when stopping loses your place.
