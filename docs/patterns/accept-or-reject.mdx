**Family:** [Select](/patterns). Choose among what exists. **Example:** Send this text, or 478 trip photos.

## When to use it

Use it for a yes or no, on one item or thousands.

## What a good design needs

A good design shows the thing itself and makes each decision one tap. A dropped item can come back.

## How it fails

It fails when the only way to review is to read every item, because then you accept them all.
