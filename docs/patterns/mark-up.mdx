**Family:** [Shape](/patterns). Change the work. **Example:** Comments on a design doc.

## When to use it

Use it to say what to change, on the exact part.

## What a good design needs

A good design makes speaking the default, and it ties each comment to its line.

## How it fails

It fails when you must describe the location in a chat message.

## Two defaults

1. **Speak first.** Dictation is the default way to respond to a document.
2. **Comments stay tied to the words.** A comment sits on the exact line it is about, so the AI knows what to change.
