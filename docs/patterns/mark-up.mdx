**Family:** [Shape](/patterns). Change the work. **Example:** Comments on a design doc.

## When to use it

Use it to say what to change, on the exact part.

## What a good design needs

A good design makes speaking the default, and it ties each comment to its line.

## How it fails

It fails when you must describe the location in a chat message.

## Two defaults

1. **Speak first.** Dictation is the default way to respond to a document.
2. **Comments stay tied to the words.** A comment sits on the exact line it is about, so the AI knows what to change.

## Calls inside a document

A document under review often needs a decision as well as edits. A comment is the wrong tool for that. It makes the person write out a choice, and it leaves the AI to work out what they meant.

So put each decision in the document as a call, at the place it comes up:

- [ ] Ask one question.
- [ ] Give at least two options, with a reason beside each.
- [ ] Put the recommended option first, and say that it is the recommendation.
- [ ] Let one tap answer it, and let a second tap change it.
- [ ] Keep a way to answer outside the options.
- [ ] Show all the calls at the end of the document, with each answer.
- [ ] Say what Approve will take. A call with no recommendation has no default, so Approve waits.

Each call is a [Pick one](/patterns/pick-one) inside the Mark up. Do not collect plain questions at the end of a document: they sit far from their reasons, and they leave no record.
