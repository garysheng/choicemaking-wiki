**Family:** [Select](/patterns). Choose among what exists. **Example:** A salary offer, or a dose.

## When to use it

Use it to choose a number on a range.

## What a good design needs

A good design shows the range, the limits and what changes as you move.

## How it fails

It fails when the AI gives one number with no range around it.
